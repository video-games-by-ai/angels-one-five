Elevation data for the "Plav & Gusinje" map
===========================================

source-assets/plav_dem.npz and the terrain generated from it
(game/assets/terrain/plav_*) are derived from Terrain Tiles, an open elevation
dataset originally produced by Mapzen and hosted by Amazon Web Services Open Data
(https://registry.opendata.aws/terrain-tiles/). The tiles used (zoom 12, around
42.6 N 19.9 E) were fetched once with tools/fetch_plav_dem.py.

On land at this zoom the dataset is built from the Shuttle Radar Topography
Mission (SRTM) survey, which is in the public domain. Credits requested by the
dataset and its sources:

* Mapzen
* United States 3DEP (formerly NED) and global GMTED2010 and SRTM terrain data
  courtesy of the U.S. Geological Survey.
* Europe terrain data produced using Copernicus data and information funded by
  the European Union - EU-DEM layers.

The full attribution list of the dataset is at
https://github.com/tilezen/joerd/blob/master/docs/attribution.md

The heights in the game are not the surveyed heights: they are measured from
Plav lake, stretched, levelled near the lake and given added detail. Do not use
them for anything but the game.
